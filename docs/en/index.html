02 / PROCESS

From facts, to evolution, back to you.

A good council has an order. It establishes the ground before debating the answer.

STEP 01

Clarify reality

Separate verified facts, assumptions, unknowns, and evidence gaps.

STEP 02

Expand the tradeoff

Compare gains, direct costs, opportunity costs, second-order effects, and reversibility.

STEP 03

Model evolution

Build baseline, upside, and downside paths with triggers, leading signals, and responses.

STEP 04

Hold a real discussion

Different perspectives disagree. You can interject and redirect before the facilitator returns the decision to you.

03 / SAFETY

Financial decisions begin with evidence and risk.

When real-time data is unavailable, the system says so. Investment maps require a data-as-of date, primary disclosures, valuation assumptions, portfolio context, and thesis-invalidating conditions.

No fabricated live data

Verify US filings with SEC EDGAR and Chinese disclosures with the exchanges or CNINFO.

No promised returns

Use ranges, conditions, and disconfirming evidence rather than a certain target price.

No delegated authority

High-stakes investment, legal, medical, and tax matters require primary-source verification and licensed professionals when appropriate.

Important: Decision Cabinet is educational decision-support software. It is not investment advice, a securities recommendation, legal advice, medical advice, or tax advice. AI can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date.
