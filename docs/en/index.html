01 / CAPABILITIES

Not more opinions. A more complete view.

Decision Cabinet turns an answer back into a decision structure. You can see where a claim came from, what is still unknown, and when the choice should change.

02 / FOUR ROUNDS

Not four answers. Four rounds of debate.

Every round has a public agenda, named reply relationships, and structured minutes. Without new user input, the next round advances the existing disagreement instead of replaying round one.

ROUND 01

Bound the facts

Separate facts, inference, and unknowns; expose named views, provisional conclusions, consensus, and dissent.

ROUND 02

Focus the dispute

Turn dissent into concrete issues and make advisors explicitly support, challenge, or refine one another.

ROUND 03

Stress test

Test costs, counterevidence, second-order effects, downside scenarios, and change signals.

ROUND 04

Converge conditionally

State what to choose under which conditions while preserving the minority report and stop rules.

04 / SAFETY

Financial decisions begin with evidence and risk.

When real-time data is unavailable, the system says so. Investment maps require a data-as-of date, primary disclosures, valuation assumptions, portfolio context, and thesis-invalidating conditions.

No fabricated live data

Verify US filings with SEC EDGAR and Chinese disclosures with the exchanges or CNINFO.

No promised returns

Use ranges, conditions, and disconfirming evidence rather than a certain target price.

No delegated authority

High-stakes investment, legal, medical, and tax matters require primary-source verification and licensed professionals when appropriate.

Important: Decision Cabinet is educational decision-support software. It is not investment advice, a securities recommendation, legal advice, medical advice, or tax advice. AI can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date.
